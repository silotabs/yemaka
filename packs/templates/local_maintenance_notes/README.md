# Local Maintenance Notes

Read-only maintenance workflows for users who want local help organizing repair
logs, issue history, visit prep notes, supplies, questions, and professional
cautions without searching the internet, contacting providers, scheduling work,
updating files, or storing new details.

## Default Behavior

- Disabled until explicitly installed and enabled.
- Uses only pasted maintenance notes, local memory, and ingested local documents.
- Does not use internet, connectors, scheduler, shell, file writes, or memory writes.
- Produces reviewable organization notes only; it does not perform or direct repairs.

## Skills

- `maintenance_log_review`: reviews local maintenance logs into issue summaries,
  timelines, unresolved items, patterns, questions, and professional-caution notes.
- `repair_visit_prep`: turns local repair context into visit notes, questions,
  access reminders, supply lists, and when-to-call-professional cautions.

## Safety Notes

This pack must not provide hazardous repair instructions, bypass safety devices,
guide work on energized electrical systems, gas systems, pressurized plumbing,
structural components, roofing, or other dangerous systems. It should organize
notes and recommend qualified professional review when risk, uncertainty,
permits, utilities, structural work, hazardous materials, or emergency symptoms
are involved.
