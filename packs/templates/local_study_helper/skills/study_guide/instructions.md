# Study Guide Workflow

## Steps

- Ask for the topic, notes, or local document scope if it is missing.
- Use rag_search for ingested notes, documents, or course material.
- Use memory_search for prior learning goals or user-provided constraints.
- Produce a compact study guide with key ideas, simple explanations, examples, misconceptions, and review checklist.
- Label each section as document-backed, memory-backed, user-provided, or inference.

## Verification Checks

- Do not use internet search or claim current external coverage.
- Do not save learning progress or personal details.
- Do not invent facts outside the provided or local source material.

## Failure Handling

- If local sources are thin, say what is missing and produce a guide only from user-provided content.
- If the topic is ambiguous, ask for the course, document, or concept to focus on.

## Example Prompts

- Make a study guide from my local notes about routing and capability packs.
