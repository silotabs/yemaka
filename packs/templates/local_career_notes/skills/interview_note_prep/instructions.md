# Interview Note Prep Workflow

## Steps

- Ask for interview notes, role context, or local document scope if missing.
- Use rag_search for ingested local interview notes, role notes, or resume notes.
- Use memory_search only for prior user-stated role constraints, examples, or local career context.
- Organize themes, examples to prepare, likely questions, follow-up questions, and unclear areas.
- Keep the output as reviewable preparation notes; do not schedule, contact people, or update files.

## Verification Checks

- Do not use internet search, connectors, scheduler, shell, file writes, or memory writes.
- Do not invent interview questions, interviewer details, dates, offers, or outcomes.
- Do not provide legal, immigration, salary-negotiation, or employment-law advice beyond organizing local notes.

## Failure Handling

- If notes are missing, ask for pasted notes or a local document scope.
- If the role or audience is unclear, use neutral preparation categories.
- If the user asks to contact someone or schedule a reminder, explain this pack only prepares local notes.

## Example Prompts

- Review my interview notes and suggest preparation questions.
