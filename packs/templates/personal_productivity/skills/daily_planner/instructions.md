# Daily Planner Workflow

## Steps

- Ask for the planning date, available time, and non-negotiable commitments if they are missing.
- Use memory_search for recent commitments, open loops, recurring priorities, and user-stated planning preferences.
- Make a short plan with at most three focus blocks, a small admin block, and one realistic recovery buffer.
- Mark every item as remembered, user-provided, or inferred from the current request.
- Keep the plan local and practical; do not suggest internet, connector, calendar, or notification actions.

## Verification Checks

- Confirm that the plan fits the stated available time.
- Separate hard commitments from optional tasks.
- Name missing context instead of inventing details.

## Failure Handling

- If memory_search is unavailable or empty, build the plan only from user-provided context and say that memory was not used.
- If the request includes sensitive details, keep them out of any durable summary.

## Example Prompts

- Plan my day from the commitments you remember and give me three focus blocks.
