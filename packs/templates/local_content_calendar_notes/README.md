# Local Content Calendar Notes

Read-only content-calendar workflows for users who want local help organizing
campaign notes, themes, content ideas, draft calendar outlines, and questions
without posting, publishing, scheduling, or connecting to marketing services.

## Default Behavior

- Disabled until explicitly installed and enabled.
- Uses only pasted content notes, local memory, and ingested local documents.
- Does not use internet, connectors, scheduler, shell, file writes, or memory writes.
- Produces reviewable notes only; it does not post, publish, schedule, send, or save content.

## Skills

- `content_calendar_outline`: organizes local campaign notes into a calendar
  outline, themes, draft slots, assumptions, and questions.
- `campaign_idea_review`: reviews local content ideas for clarity, audience fit,
  missing context, and draft next steps.

## Safety Notes

This pack must not claim current trend coverage, platform-rule compliance, ad
policy approval, legal clearance, or brand approval. Publishing, posting,
scheduling, newsletter sending, external trend lookup, media generation, and
connector actions belong outside this pack.
